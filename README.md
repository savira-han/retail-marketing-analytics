# Retail Marketing Analytics

An analysis of retail sales performance, household purchasing behavior, product performance, and marketing engagement.

## Business Overview

**DailyMart** is a grocery retailer serving household customers across multiple product categories. Its marketing activities include customer campaigns, coupons, and promotional programs designed to encourage customer engagement and purchases.

As part of the Marketing Analytics function, this analysis aims to develop a better understanding of the business's sales performance and customer behavior, providing insights to support marketing and merchandising decisions.

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on four key areas:

**Sales Performance and Growth**
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- How has household purchasing activity evolved?
- Which periods show notable changes in sales?

**Customer Purchasing Behavior**
- How frequently do households make purchases?
- Which households contribute most to sales?
- How do purchasing patterns differ across households?

**Product and Category Performance**
- Which product categories contribute most to sales?
- Which categories have the highest household participation?
- How does category performance change over time?

**Marketing and Promotional Performance**
- How do households engage with marketing campaigns?
- How does coupon redemption vary across households and products?
- How does purchasing behavior relate to recorded promotional activity?

## Project Scope

The analysis will cover:

- Sales performance and growth
- Household purchasing behavior
- Product and category performance
- Marketing and promotional engagement

The objective is to develop evidence-based insights and practical recommendations from the available data.
